Option 03 • Pro Hypertrophy & Heavy Lifting

Advanced Split Blueprint

Jinhe 1-2 saal ka continuous experience hai aur jo maximum muscle mass, density, aur strength gains target kar rahe hain.

Monday • Day 1

Heavy Chest & Anterior Delts

Kaise Karein: Barbell Bench Press (Heavy), Weighted Dips aur Cable Flyes. Failure tak sets perform karein with drop sets.

Body Changing Benefits: Chest density aur outer sweep maximize hoti hai, upper body mass extreme level par grow karta hai.

Sets & Reps5 Sets × 8, 6, 6, 4, 12 Reps
Rest Time2 Minutes
Tuesday • Day 2

Max Effort Back & Rear Delts

Kaise Karein: Deadlifts, Weighted Pull-ups aur Pendlay Rows. Form strict rakhte hue heavy weights lift karein.

Body Changing Benefits: Complete back thickness, lat spread aur spinal erector development milti hai.

Sets & Reps5 Sets × 5 to 10 Reps
Rest Time2 Minutes
Wednesday • Day 3

Quad & Calf Destruction

Kaise Karein: Heavy Barbell Squats, Hack Squats aur Leg Extensions with drop-sets.

Body Changing Benefits: Leg mass, teardrop quadriceps aur overall lower body power surge hoti hai.

Sets & Reps5 Sets × 10, 8, 8, 6, 15 Reps
Rest Time90 Seconds
Thursday • Day 4

Shoulder & Trap Specialization

Kaise Karein: Seated Dumbbell Press, Heavy Lateral Raises superset with Shrugs.

Body Changing Benefits: 3D boulder shoulders aur thick traps develop hote hain jo physique ko wide look dete hain.

Sets & Reps4 Sets × 12 Reps
Rest Time75 Seconds
Friday • Day 5

Hamstrings, Glutes & Arms

Kaise Karein: Romanian Deadlifts, Lying Leg Curls aur Barbell/Preacher Biceps Curls + Skull Crushers.

Body Changing Benefits: Arm vascularity, peak growth aur balanced lower body symmetry milti hai.

Sets & Reps4 Sets × 10 Reps
Rest Time60 Seconds
Saturday • Day 6

Pro Conditioning & Weak Point Training

Kaise Karein: Jin muscles mein growth kam lag rahi hai (jaise calves ya side delts) un par extra focus aur posing practice.

Body Changing Benefits: Physique ki perfection aur symmetry improve hoti hai.

Duration45 Minutes
FocusWeak Points & Posing
