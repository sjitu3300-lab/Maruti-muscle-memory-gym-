Option 01 • Zero to Hero

Absolute Beginner Weekly Blueprint

Agar aapne pehle kabhi gym nahi kiya, toh yeh scientifically structured program aapke neuromuscular connection, correct posture aur foundational strength ko build karega.

Monday • Day 1

Chest & Triceps (Foundation Press)

Kaise Karein: Flat Bench Press ya Push-ups se shuru karein. Chest ko upar rakhen aur elbows ko 45-degree angle par move karein.

Body Changing Benefits: Pectoral muscles active hoti hain, upper body posture sudharta hai aur triceps tone hote hain.

Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 12, 10, 8 Reps
Rest Time 90 Seconds between sets
Monday • Day 2

Back & Biceps (Pull Power)

Kaise Karein: Lat Pulldown aur Seated Cable Row ka use karein. Pull karte waqt chest ko bahar nikal kar shoulder blades ko squeeze karein.

Body Changing Benefits: Back ki width badhti hai, spinal posture strong hota hai aur back pain ki problem door hoti hai.

Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 12 Reps each
Rest Time 90 Seconds
Wednesday • Day 3

Active Rest & Mobility

Kaise Karein: Light stretching, foam rolling aur core stability plank (3 sets x 30 seconds).

Body Changing Benefits: Muscles ko recover hone ka time milta hai aur flexibility improve hoti hai.

Focus Core & Flexibility
Duration 30 Minutes Light Session
Thursday • Day 4

Legs & Lower Body Foundation

Kaise Karein: Bodyweight Squats ya Leg Press machine se start karein. Knees ko toes ki direction mein bend karein aur back straight rakhen.

Body Changing Benefits: Lower body stamina boost hota hai, glutes aur quads strong bante hain aur overall body metabolism fast hota hai.

Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 12 Reps
Rest Time 90 Seconds
Friday • Day 5

Shoulders & Abs (V-Taper Sculpting)

Kaise Karein: Dumbbell Lateral Raises aur Seated Shoulder Press lightweight ke sath perform karein.

Body Changing Benefits: Broad shoulders ka look milta hai aur core strength develop hoti hai.

Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 15 Reps
Rest Time 60 Seconds
Saturday • Day 6

Full Body Conditioning & Cardio

Kaise Karein: 20 minutes Treadmill walking/jogging + light dumbbell complexes.

Body Changing Benefits: Extra fat burn hota hai, heart health improve hoti hai aur stamina extreme level par jata hai.

Duration 30 Minutes Total
Intensity Moderate
