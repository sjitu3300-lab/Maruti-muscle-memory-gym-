Beginner Training Schedule

Apne absolute beginner level ke anusaar apne sessions ko dekhein, details padhein aur workout start karein.

Session 01

Day 1: Full Body Foundations & Stretching

Absolute beginners ke liye yeh session neuromuscular connection aur proper form build karne par focus karta hai. Isme hum body ki stiffness ko door karne ke liye dynamic stretching aur light joint mobility movements shamil karte hain, jo aage ki heavy lifting ke liye base banate hain.

Session 02

Day 2: Upper Body Mobility & Posture

Yeh workout upper body ke major muscles jaise chest, shoulders aur upper back ko activate karne ke liye design kiya gaya hai. Sahi posture maintain karte hue bodyweight aur light resistance movements ki madad se shoulders ki flexibility aur core strength mein sudhar hota hai.

Session 03

Day 3: Lower Body Strength & Stability

Lower body ke foundational movements jaise bodyweight squats aur lunges par focus karte hue yeh session leg muscles ko strength deta hai. Isme core stability aur balance ko improve karne ki exercises sikhayi jati hain taaki injury ka risk bilkul khatam ho jaye.
