Beginner Training Schedule

Aapke notebook ke anusaar Fat Loss Workout schedule. Apne session ko dekhein aur workout shuru karein.

Day 01

Day 1: Cardio, Cycling, Abs & Chest

Day 1 ki shuruat high-energy cardio jaise Jumping Jacks aur Cycling se hoti hai jo fat burning process ko fast karte hain. Iske baad core strength ke liye Abs workout aur upper body definition ke liye Chest workout shamil kiya gaya hai, jo beginners ke liye ek perfect foundation banata hai.

Day 02

Day 2: Jumping Jacks, Cycling, Abs, Back & Squats

Day 2 mein Jumping Jacks (3 sets x 1 minute) aur Cycling (5-10 minutes) ke sath endurance build kiya jata hai. Iske baad Abs aur Back workout ke sath-sath Normal Squats (3 sets x 15 reps) aur Calves (caps) training di gayi hai jo lower body ko strong banati hai.

Day 03

Day 3: Cycling, Jumping Jacks, Abs & Shoulder

Day 3 mein stamina ko maintain rakhne ke liye Cycling (5-10 minutes) aur Jumping Jacks (3 sets x 1 minute) se warm-up aur cardio kiya jata hai. Iske turant baad core strengthening ke liye Abs aur shoulder width/definition ke liye Shoulder workout perform kiya jata hai.

Day 04

Day 4: Full Upper & Lower Body Tone

Day 4 ek complete power session hai jisme Cycling (5-10 minutes) aur Jumping Jacks (3 sets x 1 minute) ke sath Abs, Biceps, Triceps, aur Forearm ki exercises shamil hain. Sath hi Normal Squats (3 sets x 15 reps) aur Calves (caps) ke sath hafte ke session ko powerful ant diya jata hai.
