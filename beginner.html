4-Day Beginner Training Blueprint

Aapke notes ke anusaar 4 din ka complete fat loss aur beginner routine. Har day par click karke detailed exercise guide dekhein.

Day 1

Cardio & Chest Ignition

Cardio [Jumping Jack] + Cycling + Abs + Chest workout. Shuruat karne ke liye best fat-burning combination.

Day 2

Lower Body & Back Power

Jumping Jack (3 sets x 1 min) + Cycling (5-10 min) + Abs + Back workout + Normal Squats (3 sets x 15 reps) + Calves.

Day 3

Core & Shoulder Sculpting

Cycling (5-10 min) + Jumping Jack (3 sets x 1 min) + Abs + Shoulder.

Day 4

Full Body Endurance & Arms

Cycling (5-10 min) + Jumping Jack (3 sets x 1 min) + Abs + Biceps + Triceps + Forearm + Normal Squats (3 sets x 15 reps) + Calves.
