Option 02 • Muscle Memory Reactivation

Restart / Intermediate Blueprint

Gap ke baad wapas aane par muscles ko shock diye bina purani form aur strength ko tezi se wapas lane ka advanced routine.

Monday • Day 1

Upper Body Hypertrophy & Re-activation

Kaise Karein: Incline Dumbbell Press aur Barbell Rows ka combination use karein. Moderate weight ke sath explosive positive aur controlled negative movement rakhen.

Body Changing Benefits: Purani muscle memory activate hoti hai, chest thickness aur back width tezi se recover hoti hai.

Sets & Reps4 Sets × 10, 8, 8, 6 Reps
Rest Time75 Seconds
Tuesday • Day 2

Lower Body Power & Core

Kaise Karein: Barbell Back Squats aur Romanian Deadlifts. Core tight rakhte hue deep descent lein.

Body Changing Benefits: Leg strength wapas aati hai aur overall hormonal release boost hota hai.

Sets & Reps4 Sets × 10 Reps
Rest Time90 Seconds
Wednesday • Day 3

Shoulder Sculpting & Arms

Kaise Karein: Overhead Military Press aur Superset Biceps/Triceps curls.

Body Changing Benefits: Arms aur shoulders mein sharp cuts wapas aane lagte hain.

Sets & Reps3 Sets × 12 Reps
RestTime60 Seconds
Thursday • Day 4

Functional Strength & Conditioning

Kaise Karein: Battle ropes, weighted lunges aur kettlebell swings.

Body Changing Benefits: Stamina improve hota hai aur stubborn fat burn hota hai.

Sets & Reps4 Rounds Circuit
Rest Time60 Seconds
Friday • Day 5

Back & Rear Delts Focus

Kaise Karein: T-Bar Rows aur Face Pulls ka heavy execution.

Body Changing Benefits: V-taper physique shape aur posture strong hota hai.

Sets & Reps4 Sets × 10 Reps
Rest Time75 Seconds
Saturday • Day 6

HIIT Cardio & Core Strength

Kaise Karein: Sprint intervals aur hanging leg raises.

Body Changing Benefits: Cardiovascular endurance aur abdominal definition milti hai.

Duration35 Minutes
IntensityHigh
