Day 1: Cardio, Cycling, Abs & Complete Chest Workout

Warm-up

CARDIO: Jumping Jacks (3 Sets x 1 Min)

Jumping Jacks ek behad prabhavshali full-body warm-up exercise hai jo aapke heart rate ko tezi se badhati hai aur poore sharir mein blood circulation ko behtar karti hai. Sharir ki stiffness ko door karke aapko aage ke heavy workouts ke liye prepare karti hai.

Cardio

CARDIO: Stationary Cycling (5 - 10 Mins)

Stationary Cycling lower body ki strength aur cardiovascular endurance ko sudharne ke liye ek shandar low-impact cardio routine hai. Isse ghutno aur joints par excessive pressure nahi padta, jabki thighs aur calves active hoti hain.

Core / Abs

CORE: Basic Crunches (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Basic Crunches core area ki rectus abdominis muscles ko target karne wali mukhya exercise hai. Yeh core strength ko mazboot banati hai, body ka posture sudhartee hai aur lower back ko support deti hai.

Chest Exercise 01

CHEST: Bench Press (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Bench Press chest building ke liye sabse mukhya compound exercise hai. Yeh pectoral muscles ke sath-sath triceps aur front shoulders ko bhi mazboot banati hai. Beginner level par proper form aur controlled movement ke sath 15 reps lagayein[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span).

Chest Exercise 02

CHEST: Incline Bench Press (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Incline Bench Press upper chest (clavicular head) ko target karti hai. Isse chest ka upper portion bharta hai aur ek attractive upper body definition milti hai. Bench ko 30-45 degree angle par set karke exercise perform karein[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

Chest Exercise 03

CHEST: Dumbbell Press (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Dumbbell Press flat bench par dumbbells ke sath ki jati hai. Isme barbell ke mukable range of motion zyada milti hai, jisse chest muscle fibers deeper stretch hote hain aur balance behtar hota hai[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).

Chest Exercise 04

CHEST: Incline Dumbbell Press (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Incline Dumbbell Press upper chest par focus karte hue independent arm movement deti hai. Yeh dono haatho ki strength ko equal maintain karne aur muscle symmetry banane ke liye behad zaroori hai[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

Chest Exercise 05

CHEST / TRICEPS: Close Grip Dumbbell Press (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Close Grip Dumbbell Press flat bench par dono dumbbells ko aapas mein touch karte hue ki jati hai. Yeh inner chest (cleavage line) aur triceps muscles ko intensely engage karti hai[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).

Chest Exercise 06

CHEST: Pec Fly / Butterfly (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Pec Fly / Butterfly chest ki finishing exercise hai jo pectoral muscles ko poori tarah isolate karke unhe stretch aur squeeze karti hai. Isse chest mein blood flow tezi se badhta hai aur muscle definition aati hai[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
