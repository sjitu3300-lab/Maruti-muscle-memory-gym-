Day 1: Cardio, Cycling, Abs & Chest

Exercise 01

CARDIO: Jumping Jacks (3 Sets x 1 Min)

Jumping Jacks ek behad prabhavshali full-body warm-up exercise hai jo aapke heart rate ko tezi se badhati hai aur poore sharir mein blood circulation ko behtar karti hai. Beginner level par yeh exercise sharir ki stiffness ko door karke aapko aage ke heavy workouts ke liye puri tarah prepare karti hai aur fat burning process ko shuru karti hai.

Exercise 02

CARDIO: Stationary Cycling (5 - 10 Mins)

Stationary Cycling lower body ki strength aur cardiovascular endurance ko sudharne ke liye ek shandar low-impact cardio routine hai. Isse ghutno aur joints par excessive pressure nahi padta, jabki thighs aur calves ki muscles active hoti hain. Yeh stamina build karne aur excess calories burn karne ke liye behad upyogi hai.

Exercise 03

CORE / ABS: Basic Crunches (3 Sets x 15 Reps)

Basic Crunches core area ki rectus abdominis muscles ko target karne wali sabse mukhya exercise hai. Yeh core strength ko mazboot banati hai, body ka posture sudhartee hai aur lower back support deti hai. Perform karte samay apni core muscles ko squeeze karein aur slow controlled movement rakhein taaki behtar results mil sakein.

Exercise 04

CHEST / UPPER BODY: Wall Pushups (3 Sets x 12-15 Reps)

Wall Pushups beginners ke liye chest, shoulders aur triceps ki upper body strength build karne ka ek surakshit aur prabhavshali tarika hai. Floor pushups karne se pehle yeh exercise joints par load ko control karte hue muscles ko correct form sikhati hai. Regular practice se arm strength aur shoulder stability mein tezi se sudhar hota hai.
